Pen Pal 2.2.0

- The fee budget is now monthly. Set an amount and it applies from that month on; change it later and earlier months keep the budget they had. The warning when you log a fee checks against that month.
- The Fee budget page shows one month at a time, with the year's chart below it. Click a month in the chart to open it. A dashed line marks each month's budget.
- If you had a yearly budget, it carries over as a twelfth of it each month, starting that January.
